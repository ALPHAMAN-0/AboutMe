AI résumé builder with an ATS score

Scores a résumé from 0 to 100 against a job description, lists the keywords it is missing and drafts a summary with Claude. React, TypeScript and Zustand, with no backend.

Email Siam Open the live demo

How the ATS score adds up to 100

A heuristic, as the app itself says: it does not promise that every ATS will parse a résumé the same way.

  • Section coverage

    Experience, education, skills and projects are all there

    20
  • Action verbs

    Bullet points start with what you did

    20
  • Quantified results

    Numbers in the bullets, not only duties

    20
  • Contact and keywords

    Contact details, and the job’s keywords found in the text

    15 + 15
  • Format and length

    A length and layout an ATS can read

    10

How I use models

I treat a model’s output as a draft: Sleep Loop waits for a person to approve before it merges, and Gridwise checks an LLM’s plan against hard limits before a solver runs it.
