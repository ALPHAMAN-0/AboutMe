Client project · in daily use at the counter

Pharmacy POS for Nasema Pharmacy

Billing, stock, prescriptions, invoices and reports in one web app, with a login for each kind of staff. I built it on Node.js and MongoDB for a pharmacy counter in Bangladesh.

Email Siam See how a sale works

How a sale moves through the POS

  1. The sale at the counter

    Search by brand, generic name or barcode, build the cart, then take cash, bKash, Nagad or card.

  2. Stock by batch and expiry

    Every batch carries its expiry date. Stock near it turns amber, stock past it turns red.

  3. An invoice for every sale

    Each sale gets a numbered invoice with its payment method, and a refund stays on the record.

  4. Reports by date

    Sales, expiry, stock value and regulatory reports for any range of days.

Medicine records and printed receipts

A record for every medicine

Brand, generic name, strength, pack size, rack, cost and sale price, a low-stock level and an Rx flag.

A receipt with the licence number

Receipts print the pharmacy’s drug licence number, the items, the payment method and the owner’s footer line.

What runs underneath the POS

The owner sets the expiry alert window, the tax rate and the receipt footer from a settings page.
